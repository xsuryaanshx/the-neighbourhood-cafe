# neighbourhood · cafe & garden bistro · indore

A bespoke, editorial digital experience crafted specifically for **"neighbourhood" (Neighbourhood Cafe, Saket, Indore)**. 

Designed and engineered directly from the physical cafe's architecture, customer photographs, and verified 4.6★ Google reviews (1,400+ reviews).

---

## 🏛️ Architectural Fidelity & Extracted Color Palette

Rather than generic templates or synthetic neon gradients, the design palette and typography are derived directly from the real architectural spaces captured in the cafe's photography:

1. **The Glasshouse Solarium (Upstairs)**: High natural daylight roof over French woven cane bistro chairs and muted celadon checkerboard tile floor (`#98A79B`).
2. **The Stucco Bungalow & Wrought-Iron Gate**: Whitewashed Mediterranean lime-wash stucco walls (`#F4EFEA`) with the signature quiet lowercase `neighbourhood` typography and botanical mint-green entrance gate (`#B5C7BB`).
3. **The Stepped Courtyard (Outdoor Amphitheater)**: Underlit warm golden amber slats (`#D5A056`) beneath outdoor amphitheater masonry seating and lush frangipani greenery (`#3B4E3E`).
4. **Primary Ink**: Deep charcoal espresso (`#231C18`) providing crisp editorial contrast without pure black harshness.

## 🍽️ Verified Culinary Highlights (Indore Guest Favorites)

The interactive menu features real crowd favorites verified from local Indore customer reviews:
- **Mushroom Stroganoff & Herb Rice**: Indore's most reviewed comfort main — wild sautéed mushrooms in sour cream paprika reduction with butter rice.
- **Parmesan & Black Truffle Hand-Cut Fries**: Tossed in Italian cold-pressed truffle oil, thyme salt, and aged parmesan.
- **Biscoff Anjeer Thick Milkshake**: Indore's signature fusion pairing caramelized Lotus Biscoff biscuit spread with Afghani sun-cured figs.
- **Creamy Truffle & Wild Shiitake Tagliatelle**: Handcrafted broad ribbon pasta in white truffle butter emulsion.
- **Burrata & San Marzano Artisan Pizza**: Fermented sourdough crust, San Marzano tomato base, fresh torn burrata, and basil oil.
- **Beirut Falafel & Whipped Garlic Hummus**: Silk chickpea dip with hot pita and herb falafels.
- **100% Jain Friendly Filter**: Instant toggle highlighting root-free delicacies and custom Jain preparations.

## ⚡ Key Features

- **Architectural Zone Table Booking**: Guests can select their preferred space (The Glasshouse Solarium, The Stepped Courtyard, or The Stucco Bungalow) with direct WhatsApp confirmation to the host desk.
- **Slide-Over Food Tray (Cart)**: Guests can build their order, adjust quantities, see instant totals, and dispatch orders directly to the cafe's WhatsApp counter.
- **Direct Google Maps Integration**: Direct links to the verified Google Maps listing: [share.google/zofWieIM0p626tVCU](https://share.google/zofWieIM0p626tVCU).
- **Zero AI Slop**: No gradients, no neon glow, no synthetic badge clutter, no emojis in headings. Pure editorial sophistication with Cormorant Garamond serif and Plus Jakarta Sans.
